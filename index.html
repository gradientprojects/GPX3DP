    Tips for a clean deboss
    • Grooves at least 1.5 nozzle widths wide, plastic between them at least 1.75. The letters are gaps in the first layers: first-layer squish closes narrow ones, and the thin plastic inside letters like e and a fills in first. The readout above measures your text.
    • Elephant foot compensation in the slicer (Bambu's default is 0.15 mm) shrinks the first layer, which widens the letters. If the text prints fat, lower it; if the letters fill in, raise it.
    • A smooth plate gives crisp, glossy text. A textured plate makes it harder to read.
    • 3 or 4 layers deep reads well. Deeper only adds shadow, and the top of each letter is bridged.
    • Bold, simple fonts. For a long name, pick a condensed font rather than a smaller size.
    • For contrast, rub paint or a paint marker into the letters, then wipe the surface clean.
